Featured project

Nova

A quantitative research system designed to be allowed to say “I don't know.”

Nova is an independent Python/FastAPI platform for investigating whether trading strategies have defensible evidence. It preserves the distinction between missing data and zero, partial and complete success, research and authorization.

The audited system included a seven-stage governed pipeline, real Interactive Brokers market data, paper execution, explicit provenance, fail-closed risk gates, and approximately 2,900 passing tests.

Engineering approach

Trust is a system property.

01

Trace the boundary

Find the first place correct information becomes incorrect instead of changing the component nearest the symptom.

02

Make unknown visible

Missing, degraded, and partial states should travel downstream rather than becoming plausible values.

03

Test the handoff

Unit tests protect components; integration tests reveal whether the whole path honors its contract.
